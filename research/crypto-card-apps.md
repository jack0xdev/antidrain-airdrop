# Crypto card / stablecoin neobank apps — full list

Same type as the apps in your folder (Fasset, Avalanche Card, Mypal, Tria, Gluon, Plasma One): top up crypto or stablecoins, spend with a Visa/Mastercard.

- **142 cards** from [awesome-crypto-cards](https://github.com/mbtrilla/awesome-crypto-cards) (commit `bed8d0b`, Sep 30 2026) — only cards that can be ordered today; closed or announced-only cards are left out.
- **8 more** found by web search that are not in that list.
- **Vantage is not this type.** It is a CFD/forex broker (Vantage Markets) that accepts USDT deposits — no crypto card.
- ⭐ = already in your folder.

New card apps launch every month, so no list is ever 100% complete. Always open official sites from the app store or a bookmark — fake card sites are used for wallet drainers.
